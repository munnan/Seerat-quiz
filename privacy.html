Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Seerat Quiz collects, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Seerat Quiz application and website.

1. About Seerat Quiz

Seerat Quiz is an educational quiz application designed to help users create and use quizzes based on Seerat-related educational content. The application may use Google Forms to create and manage quiz forms when the user chooses to connect their Google account.

2. Google Account and Google Forms Data

If you authorize Seerat Quiz to connect to your Google account, the application may request access to Google Forms through the following Google OAuth scope:

Google Forms API scope:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/forms.body

This permission allows Seerat Quiz to create and modify the content (body) of Google Forms as required for the quiz-generation features that you explicitly use.

Seerat Quiz requests this permission only when it is required for the relevant functionality. We do not request Google user-data permissions that are unrelated to the application's features.

3. How Google Data Is Used

Information accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, including creating or modifying Google Forms used for Seerat Quiz quizzes.

Google user data is not used for advertising, targeted advertising, selling user information, or building user profiles unrelated to the requested application functionality.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use

Seerat Quiz's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Google user data is accessed and used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and the functionality presented to the user. We do not sell Google user data or use Google user data for personalized or interest-based advertising.

5. Information We May Access

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Seerat Quiz may process:

  • Google account information required by the Google authorization flow.
  • Google Forms information necessary to create or modify quiz forms.
  • Quiz content and settings that you provide through Seerat Quiz.

Seerat Quiz does not intentionally request access to unrelated Google services or information.

6. Information Storage

Seerat Quiz does not intentionally maintain a permanent copy of Google user data for purposes unrelated to the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

Where information must temporarily pass through the application to perform an operation requested by the user, it is handled only as necessary to provide that operation.

7. Sharing of Information

Seerat Quiz does not sell, rent, or trade Google user data.

Google data is not shared with advertising networks, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or unrelated profiling purposes. Information may be processed by Google services themselves when you use Google Forms and Google authorization features, subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

8. User Control and Revoking Access

You choose whether to authorize Seerat Quiz to access your Google account and Google Forms.

You can revoke Seerat Quiz's Google account access at any time through your Google Account security settings. After authorization is revoked, Seerat Quiz will no longer be able to use the revoked Google permissions.

9. Deletion of Data

If you want information associated with your use of Seerat Quiz to be deleted, please use the contact method provided on the Seerat Quiz website and describe the information you want removed.

Google Forms created in your Google account remain under your control and can be deleted by you from Google Forms or Google Drive, subject to Google's services and retention policies.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Seerat Quiz. Communications with our website and services should use secure HTTPS connections.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

11. Children's Privacy

Seerat Quiz is an educational application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted where applicable.

12. Third-Party Services

Seerat Quiz may use Google services, including Google Forms and Google OAuth, to provide its functionality. Your use of those Google services is also subject to Google's policies and terms.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Seerat Quiz handles information, please contact the Seerat Quiz developer through the contact information provided with the application or on the Seerat Quiz website.
